About Our Project

Learning about the importance of herbal plants in our daily lives.

Herbal plants are an important part of nature. They have been used in traditional practices for generations. This project introduces different herbal plants, their characteristics, traditional uses and methods of cultivation. Our aim is to create awareness about protecting and growing useful plants.
